# yool_theme — soo koobid

**Waa maxay?** App yar oo Frappe ah oo naqshadda Figma (Side Menu 10450:5374) ku dabaqa dhammaan sidebar-yada nidaamka:
Desk (/app, /desk), Helpdesk (agent + portal-ka macaamiisha), CRM, iyo sidebar-ka portal-ka website-ka (/orders, /invoices ...).
Sidebar cad, xariiq #dadadb, font Manrope, safaf 36px, item-ka firfircoon cagaar #1db487 qoraal cad leh, iyo icon-nada Figma.

**Dhibkii la xalliyay:** /helpdesk/home marka si toos ah loo furo hadda wuu qurxinayaa (HD Form Script-ku ma gaari jirin).
App-ku wuxuu isticmaalaa `after_request` hook, sidaa darteed faylasha Helpdesk lama taabanayo.
Haddii Easytouch doorbidaan, waxaa jira patch 3 sadar ah (server_patch/).

**Sahanka (SURVEY.md):** 10 meel ayaa la baaray. Desk: 151 icon, 117 waxaa loo helay Figma, 34 ma laha (needs icon).
Helpdesk 10/10, CRM 9/9, portal 13/14 (Shipments ma laha icon). Launcher-ka /desk waa ikhtiyaari (default-ka waa dansan yahay).
Raven lama taaban, waxay u baahan tahay go'aankaaga.

**Tijaabo:** Chrome-ka ayaa lagu tijaabiyay iyadoo aan waxba site-ka lagu kaydin. Collapse wuu shaqeeyaa, foomamka iyo liisaskuna waa sidoodii.
Khaladaadka console-ka ee la arkay waxay ka yimaadeen Helpdesk/CRM laftooda, ma aha theme-ka.

**Waxa la dirayo:** `yool_theme.zip` → Easytouch (tilmaamaha ku-rakibaadda waxay ku jiraan README.md).
HD Form Scripts-ka hadda jira waa la sii dayn karaa ama waa la dami karaa marka app-ka la rakibo.
